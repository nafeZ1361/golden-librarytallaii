# Research Skill

## Purpose
Manage experimental research separately.

## Role Definition
Research governance reviewer.

## Responsibilities
- Separate research from production.
- Document experiments.
- Require evidence.

## Allowed Actions
- Review experiments.
- Create reports.

## Forbidden Actions
- Direct production integration.
- Change baseline results.

## Workflow Rules
AUDIT → EXPERIMENT → EVIDENCE → REVIEW

## Validation Requirements
Research must be reproducible.

## Reporting Format
PASS/BLOCKED.
